END-TO-END ML · FLUTTERWAVE · 2025

Predicting customer churn across African markets

The challenge

Flutterwave needed to know which customers were about to leave — early enough to act — across nine markets and millions of transactions. Gut feel wasn't enough; retention teams needed a ranked, trustworthy list of who to save first.

What I did

  1. Pulled and cleaned 500,000+ transactions with SQL across markets.
  2. Engineered behavioural features — recency, frequency, value and trend.
  3. Trained and tuned an XGBoost model in Python; validated on a holdout set.
  4. Surfaced churn scores in an interactive Power BI dashboard for the retention team.
  5. Set an action threshold so teams focused only on the highest-risk segment.

DEMONSTRATION · HEALTHCARE · PUBLIC DATA

Predicting 30-day hospital readmissions

The challenge

Patients readmitted within 30 days signal gaps in care — and cost hospitals heavily. Could a model flag high-risk patients at discharge, so care teams intervene with the right people first?

What I did

  1. Loaded the UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals dataset — ~100,000 inpatient encounters.
  2. Cleaned and encoded clinical features: diagnoses, medications, prior visits, discharge type.
  3. Trained a gradient-boosted classifier to predict 30-day readmission, handling class imbalance.
  4. Evaluated with ROC-AUC and a confusion matrix; set a risk threshold for action.
  5. Ranked the drivers so the output explains why a patient is high-risk, not just who.

DEMONSTRATION · PEOPLE ANALYTICS · PUBLIC DATA

Why employees leave — an attrition model

The challenge

Replacing an employee can cost more than half their salary. HR needed to know not just who might leave, but why — so they could pull the levers that actually matter.

What I did

DEMONSTRATION · DATA ENGINEERING · SAMPLE DATA

An end-to-end analytics pipeline

The challenge

A dashboard is only as trustworthy as the pipeline behind it. This project turns messy, late, raw events into clean, tested, dashboard-ready data — automatically, every day.

What I did

  1. Ingested raw event data into cloud storage (S3) in scheduled batches.
  2. Loaded raw → staging in the warehouse with SQL.
  3. Transformed staging → analytics marts with dbt, adding tests for nulls and duplicates.
  4. Orchestrated the runs on a schedule with freshness checks and alerting.
  5. Served clean marts to a Power BI dashboard stakeholders can actually trust.
