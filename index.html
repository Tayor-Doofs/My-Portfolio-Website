About

I'm Odutayo — a data analyst in Lagos with six years turning FinTech data into decisions.

Most of that time has been at companies like Flutterwave and First Bank, building the dashboards, churn and risk models, and data pipelines that help teams see what's actually happening — and do something about it. The part I enjoy most is the unglamorous bit: cleaning the mess, asking the awkward question, and getting to an answer a business can move on.

Today I lead Data & Insights at PESA, where I own the reporting that drives product and growth decisions. I'm a Microsoft Certified Data Analyst and AWS Cloud Practitioner, with an MSc in Information Technology from the University of Lagos and a background in Computer Science.

If you've got a data problem that's been nagging you, I'd genuinely like to hear about it.
