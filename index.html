About me

I'm Odutayo — a data analyst, a teacher, and the founder of Doofstech.

By day I lead Data & Insights at PESA, after six years building dashboards, churn and risk models, and data platforms at Flutterwave and First Bank. The work I love most is the unglamorous part: cleaning the mess and getting to an answer a business can act on.

I also teach — Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel and automation, beginner to advanced, at Dataleum, Byte Analytics, and one-on-one. Passing it on is half the fun.

Away from the screen, I'm into football and music. They're not my job — just the things that keep me human when the dashboards get heavy.
